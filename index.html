The real barrier

You're not scared of moving abroad. You're scared of being nobody for 18 months.

At home you're the funny one, the competent one, the one who knows how everything works. Abroad you're the person who can't order bread, can't sign a lease, can't get hired without an address — and can't get an address without a job. That loop is the whole problem. Breaking it is the whole job.

The timeline nobody warns you about

Every move runs the same three phases. Most people quit in the second one — right before it gets real.

MONTH 01

The holiday

MONTH 03

The crisis

The decision
